About Our Company

Our Story & Leadership

At the heart of FXR Construction Inc. and Central Valley Turnkey Services (CVTS) is a commitment to people. Our founder and General Contractor, Jesus Robles, has dedicated over three decades to the construction industry. His career hasn't just been about building structures; it’s been about building a reputation as a man of reliable character and genuine helpfulness.

For Jesus, "turnkey service" isn't just a business model—it’s a personal promise to take the weight off the client's shoulders. He understands that property managers and owners need more than just a contractor; they need a partner they can trust implicitly. Known for his responsible nature and deep sense of integrity, Jesus approaches every project with the goal of being a steady, helpful hand for his clients.

Today, CVTS carries forward that 30-year legacy, bridging the gap between high-level general contracting and the urgent, day-to-day needs of shopping centers, mobile home parks, and office complexes. When you work with us, you are working with decades of experience and a leader who stands firmly behind his word.
